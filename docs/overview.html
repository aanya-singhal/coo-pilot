CoO-PILOT

Evidence-backed pre-clearance for Certificate of Origin claims. AI reads the documents; deterministic logic decides; a human signs off.
SIH 2026  ·  IHSIH024  ·  Team Null Pointers  ·  Prototype

The problem

A preferential-tariff claim under a trade agreement is checked by hand. An officer reads a commercial invoice, a packing list and a cost statement, checks they agree, and decides whether the goods meet the agreement's rules of origin. It is slow, inconsistent between officers, and leaves no structured record of why a claim was cleared. CoO-PILOT prepares the case and shows its working. It does not issue certificates.

Stages 1–2 are AI. Stages 3–6 are deterministic code. Stage 7 is a person. The model reads; it never decides. A misread field surfaces as a reconciliation mismatch and routes to review rather than passing through unnoticed.

What the system decides

OutcomeWhenWorked example
APPROVEDDocuments reconcile and the origin criterion is met Invoice and packing list agree at 500 units; RVC 42.86% against the AIFTA 35% threshold; all non-originating materials change tariff sub-heading. Risk 0.
PENDING_REVIEWAnything unresolved — a discrepancy, or origin that cannot be evaluated Invoice says 500 units, packing list says 480. Origin still passes, but the documents disagree, so it is held for a human. Risk 40.
REJECTEDOrigin was evaluated and failed Same documents, non-originating value 3500: RVC 16.67%, below the threshold.

A document discrepancy never auto-rejects — it routes to a person, which is what an officer would do.

The refusal case An invoice and a packing list cannot establish origin. Regional value content needs a cost statement: FOB value plus the value and classification of every non-originating material — the information CAROTAR 2020 requires an importer to hold in Form I. Without it the engine returns INSUFFICIENT_DATA and names the missing fields rather than estimating a percentage. Saying "I don't know" is a feature, not a gap.

Rules of origin — and their limits

Thresholds are stored as data with a citation and source URL attached to each, and the engine reports which criterion it applied so a reviewer can check the working.

AgreementValue contentTariff shiftBasis
AIFTA (ASEAN–India)≥ 35% of FOBCTSH · 6-digit AIFTA Rules of Origin, Rule 4. Given effect in India by Notification 189/2009-Cus (N.T.), 31.12.2009.
SAFTA (non-LDC)≥ 40% of FOBCTH · 4-digitSAFTA Rules of Origin, twin criteria.
SAFTA (LDC)≥ 30% of FOBCTH · 4-digitTen points below the non-LDC threshold.

Regional value content is computed by the indirect (build-down) method: (FOB − non-originating value) ÷ FOB × 100. Goods declared wholly obtained skip both tests.

Stated limits
  • General rule only. Product-specific rules override it for many tariff lines and are not implemented.
  • Thresholds verified against secondary sources (national trade portals), not gazette text. Verify before operational use.
  • Risk weights are ours, a triage aid with no statutory basis. The interface says so on screen.
  • Three agreement variants of India's many.

Auditability

Every claim carries a full trail — claim_created, document_uploaded, extraction_started / completed / failed, reconciliation_completed, processing_completed, and the officer's approved / rejected / information_requested. Credentials are never written to it.

Each extracted field records the document it came from, so any value in a decision can be traced back to the page it was read from. Officer decisions are stored with reviewer and comments.
